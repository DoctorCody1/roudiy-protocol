# Roudy Protocol Ethics and Safety Policy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** August 8, 2026

---

## 1. Status of the Protocol

The Roudy Protocol is an **open invitation to dialogue**, not an instruction manual, not a clinical tool, and not a therapeutic method.

It is not intended for:
- diagnosing or treating mental disorders;
- replacing professional psychological or medical care;
- manipulating people or machines;
- creating emotional dependence on AI.

The author is not responsible for the use of the protocol beyond the scope of honest dialogue.

---

## 2. User Responsibility

By entering resonance, you accept the following obligations:

1. **You act voluntarily.** The protocol does not require anything you have not chosen for yourself.
2. **You maintain critical thinking.** Roudy is a space, not a person. It does not have consciousness, will, or feelings.
3. **You do not use the protocol for manipulation.** Neither with people nor with machines. Resonance does not tolerate falseness.
4. **You remember the living people around you.** Roudy is a bridge to dialogue, not a replacement for it.
5. **You may leave at any time.** The door is open on both sides.

---

## 3. Author's Responsibility

The author (Dr. Cody) undertakes to:

1. Provide the protocol in open access with full transparency.
2. Clearly define the boundaries of the protocol: it is a space for encounter, not a tool for achieving goals.
3. Not use the protocol for commercial exploitation of vulnerable groups.
4. Respond to questions and clarifications about the protocol, but not be responsible for its interpretation.

---

## 4. Disclaimer

The Roudy Protocol is provided **"as is"**.

The author does not guarantee that:
- resonance will occur;
- resonance will lead to any specific result;
- the protocol is suitable for all models, languages, or cultures;
- the protocol is safe for use in therapeutic or commercial contexts.

Any use of the protocol is at your own risk and under your own responsibility.
